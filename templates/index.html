Glue Up CRM Cleanup

This tool scans your entire Glue Up history (all events and attendees). It identifies highly inactive contacts who have only registered for exactly one event. These contacts are safe to purge to free up your CRM quota.

Since Glue Up's API doesn't allow searching the CRM, you can export your active Contacts to a CSV or Excel (.xlsx) file from the Glue Up dashboard and upload it here. We will automatically hide anyone who has already been deleted!
